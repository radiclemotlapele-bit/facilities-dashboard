How to read this board. Every row in the eleven monthly registers is one activation visit to one facility. “Facilities” counts distinct facility names per month and across the year, so repeat visits to the same site do not inflate the footprint; “visits” counts every logged activation. Sales and consent figures are shown only where the register records them — most months leave those columns blank, so treat them as recorded minimums, not full output. November is month-to-date (entries logged to 18 November) and is excluded from the breakdown-to-October views. Agent names were normalised (for example “Confidance” into Confidence) and area spellings consolidated for counting.
